# Image rights

All imported location photos have `rights_status: unverified`. Source URLs and photographer hints are documented in IMAGE-SOURCES.json and in the respective CMS gallery. No license or permission was inferred from public availability. Obtain permission or replace photographs before public use. Images are visible for review (`publish: true`); turning off `publish` removes them from the rendered gallery. These fields are intentionally independent so the content owner controls publication.

The photo selection is location-specific, not a verified Google Business Profile top-five ranking. No ongoing Google photo integration or API credential is required or included.
